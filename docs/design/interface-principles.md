# Interface principles

1. **One primary action per view.** Everything else recedes.
2. **Typography is the product.** A reader is a typographic interface; the rest
   is chrome and should behave like it.
3. **The mono voice carries the personality.** Labels, timecodes and counts are
   monospaced. Prose never is, unless the reader asks for it.
4. **One accent.** Amber marks action and position. State is otherwise carried
   by weight, ground and placement, not by more hues.
5. **No sidebar, at any width.** A reader is not a dashboard. Desktop earns its
   space with a grid, a wider measure and longer rails.
6. **Empty states speak the lexicon.** `Nothing here.` / `Add something.`
7. **Quiet motion.** Short, eased, and gone. The focus word replaces; it never
   slides.
8. **Contrast over atmosphere.** Gradients decorate the onboarding ground and
   nothing else.
9. **Honesty over claims.** No "3× faster, with science". Focus mode says what
   it does and what it costs.
10. **Do not look like the benchmark.** No red, no bookmark mark, no science
    onboarding. The interaction grammar is shared; the identity is not.
