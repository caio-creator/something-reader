# Roadmap

Status as of the September 2026 audit and the work that followed it. Things that
shipped are not listed as future work.

**Done** — browser app and the magic moment; EPUB, PDF, DOCX, Markdown, HTML,
text, paste and web link; chapter contents; title and metadata search; PWA with
a versioned offline shell; Listen, through the device's voices and through
Something Voice.

**Next** — backup and restore of the library, versioned, because it protects the
one thing a reader cannot get back. Then simple bookmarks, and ordering by what
was read recently.

**After evidence** — collections and full-text search, once a library large
enough to need them has been tried.

**Not scheduled** — Tauri, OCR, sync, AI providers. None of them addresses
anything the audit found.

**Open before a wide release** — Something Voice reaching confirmed audio across
machines, a real iOS device, and a first offline install measured on a phone. See
`docs/reviews/app-audit.md`.
